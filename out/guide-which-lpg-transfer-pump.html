Which LPG transfer pump do you need?

A plain comparison of the five YAM pumps, so you can choose by what you fill, the power you have and how often you use it.

Start with three questions

  • What do you fill? A car tank, or small cylinders from a big one.
  • What power do you have? A car battery, a 220V socket, a 110V socket, or no electricity.
  • How often? Now and then for yourself, or all day as a business.

Our advice

  • Filling your own car: the 12V DC pump. It runs from the car battery, is the lightest to ship and works in every country.
  • Your country uses 110V: choose the 12V DC pump. The 220V pumps are only for 220V mains.
  • Filling small cylinders at home or in a shop: the 220V AC pump.
  • A business filling all day: the 1 HP commercial pump, or the 1 HP belt-drive pump if you want to use a motor you already have.
  • No electricity: the manual pump. Speed depends on the person pumping.

What every pump includes

Every YAM pump handles up to 22 kg/cm² (21.5 bar) and arrives ready to use, with hoses, valves and spare parts in the box. The diaphragm is the part that wears; spares are included and it is easy to replace yourself. All pumps have a one-year guarantee.

Check the law in your country. Rules for transferring LPG between cylinders and cars are different in every country. Please check the rules where you live before you start. We export only and do not sell these pumps in India.
