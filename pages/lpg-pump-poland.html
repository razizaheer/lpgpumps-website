Shipping to Poland

LPG transfer pumps delivered to Poland.

We send YAM LPG and propane transfer pumps from India to Poland. Order one pump or more. Each one arrives ready to use, with its hoses, valves and spare parts.

Power in Poland

Mains supply in Poland is 230 V, 50 Hz. Our AC pumps are made for 220 V, so tell us your supply and we will confirm the right pump. The 12V DC pump runs from any car battery.

Delivery

We ship pumps to Poland. We send single pumps mostly by EMS Speed Post, within 4 to 5 days of payment. DHL, FedEx or UPS are possible for an extra cost.

Price and payment

The price depends on the pump and the quantity. Message us and we reply with one total price, shipping to Poland included. Pay by bank transfer or PayPal.

Guarantee and spares

One-year guarantee on manufacturing defects. The diaphragm is the part that wears; spares come in the box and it is easy to change.

Check the law in your country. Rules for moving LPG between cylinders and cars are different in every country. Please check your local rules before you order. We export only and do not sell these pumps in India.
