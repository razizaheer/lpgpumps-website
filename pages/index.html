What is the price?

The price depends on your country and how many pumps you want. Send us a WhatsApp message and we reply with one total price, pump and shipping together.

Will it fit my cylinder and my car?

Cylinder and car connectors are different in every country. The pump comes with its hoses and valves, and you add the connector that is used where you live.

How long until I receive it?

We send the pump within 4 to 5 days of your payment, mostly by EMS Speed Post, and our customers have not had problems receiving their pumps. DHL, FedEx, UPS or TNT are possible for an extra cost.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to our company account, or by PayPal.

What if a part breaks?

The part that wears is the diaphragm. Spares come in the box and it is easy to change. The pump also has a one-year guarantee for manufacturing defects, and we keep every part in stock.
